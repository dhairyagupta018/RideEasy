# RideEasy

**Rent a car, bike or scooter, all in one place.**

RideEasy is a vehicle rental website that makes it simple to find a vehicle, book it and keep track of your rental.

**Visit the website:** https://dhairyagupta018.github.io/RideEasy/

---

## What You Can Do on RideEasy

- **Choose your ride:** browse cars, bikes and scooters in one place.
- **Check availability:** see which vehicles are free before you book.
- **Create an account:** register quickly with a simple sign-up form.
- **Book in minutes:** pick a vehicle, fill in your details and confirm your booking.
- **Pay with ease:** complete your payment details as part of the booking.
- **Track your rental:** keep an eye on your current and past rentals.
- **Stay informed:** receive notifications about your bookings and rentals.

---

## How It Works

1. **Sign up:** open the website and register for a free account.
2. **Find a vehicle:** browse the listings and choose a car, bike or scooter that suits you.
3. **Check availability:** make sure your chosen vehicle is available.
4. **Book it:** fill in the booking form with your rental details.
5. **Pay:** complete the payment step to confirm your booking.
6. **Ride:** pick up your vehicle and enjoy your trip.
7. **Track:** view your rental status and history any time.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need to install anything?**
No. RideEasy runs in your web browser.

**Do I need an account to book?**
Yes. Register once, then use your account for all your bookings.

**Can I see which vehicles are available?**
Yes. Availability is shown on the vehicle listings.

**Can I check my past rentals?**
Yes. Rental tracking shows your current and previous rentals.
